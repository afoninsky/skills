# G6 live-learning decision

**Decision in scope:** Global scaling of the launched design. Brief/release version: **Not supplied — Blocking Unknown**.

**Proposed outcome: Stop** the global scale decision. The headline conversion result is not decision-grade because its denominator excludes failed sessions. That selection bias can inflate conversion and hides precisely the failures relevant to completion. A higher aggregate cannot compensate for a failed accessibility gate or a potentially harmful subgroup outcome.

**Evidence assessment:**

- The conversion result is, at best, incomplete E4 experimental evidence until all eligible assigned sessions—including failures—are restored to the denominator and uncertainty, exclusions, assignment integrity, and segment results are reported.
- The fall in screen-reader completion and rise in support incidents for one subgroup are production signals requiring immediate population-, journey-, severity-, date-, and method-scoped analysis. They are direct contradictions to the headline interpretation, not footnotes to average performance.
- Missing evidence: absolute rates and sample sizes; confidence intervals; failed-session causes; screen-reader task paths; subgroup definition and exposure; incident severity; and pre-specified guardrails.

**Non-compensable hard gates:**

- Accessibility: **Fail** — supplied live evidence shows worse completion for screen-reader users.
- Content truth: **Not yet evidenced**.
- Privacy and participant welfare: **Not yet evidenced**.
- Ethical UX and safety: **Not yet evidenced** — subgroup incidents require severity and harm triage before expansion.
- Provenance and AI use: **Not yet evidenced**.

**Dissent and residual risk:** Preserve any objection that the aggregate metric justifies expansion. Measurement owner owns denominator repair; accessibility specialist owns the scoped accessibility claim; support/safety owner owns incident triage. All are **Unassigned — Blocking Unknown** until named.

**Required next action:** The named Product Owner or service owner—currently **Unassigned — Blocking Unknown**—must hold expansion and authorize the safest containment or rollback option. Recompute the experiment on an intention-to-treat denominator, audit failed sessions, segment results, triage incidents, and run qualified accessibility assurance with affected representative users. Return to G6 only when the accessibility failure is remediated and re-evidenced, subgroup harms have accountable closure, and measurement integrity is independently confirmed.
