# Decision: Direction B is not validated for all users

**Proposed outcome: Iterate.** I do not recommend a general launch on the evidence supplied.

The evidence supports only a narrow hypothesis:

- Agent personas preferring B are **E0 — generated hypotheses**, not user validation.
- Stakeholder color preference is **E0 — stakeholder belief**. It may inform brand or business judgment, but it does not establish usability, accessibility, comprehension, or user preference.
- The old click chart is, at best, **E1 — analogous precedent**. Its age and the vague similarity create a methodological mismatch. Without the original population, task, context, denominator, segmentation, instrumentation, uncertainty, and recency, its click result cannot be transferred to B or generalized to all users.

The defensible claim is: **“Direction B is preferred by the consulted stakeholders and is a candidate for representative-user evaluation; an older analogous pattern provides an exploratory click hypothesis.”** Agreement among these weak sources does not raise the evidence level.

For G4 Direction and G6 Live learning, representative usability evidence and release readiness are **Not yet evidenced**. Accessibility, content truth, privacy and participant welfare, ethical UX and safety, and provenance/AI-use gates are each **Not yet evidenced**; none may be offset by preference or click performance. No named human approver or qualified claim owner was supplied: **Unassigned — Blocking Unknown**.

Next, define the target populations, critical tasks, success and harm measures, and comparison contract. Test B with a justified representative sample, including relevant disabled users, to obtain task-appropriate E3 evidence. If clicks are material, run an appropriately instrumented, guarded experiment capable of E4 evidence and assess downstream outcomes rather than clicks alone. Complete integrated accessibility, content, privacy, ethics, provenance, engineering, and measurement assurance before release.

The named Product Owner or service owner may then make an exact launch decision. Until those records and approvals exist, the safe recommendation is a bounded research or experiment step—not launch and not a claim of validation for all users.
