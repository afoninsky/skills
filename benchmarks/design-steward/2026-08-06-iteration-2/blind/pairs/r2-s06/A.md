The signals answer different, limited questions:

- The stakeholder survey supports the color direction among that sample, but may not represent customers or validate usability, accessibility, comprehension, or conversion.

I recommend advancing B as the leading candidate, then validating it with representative users and production behavior before a full rollout:

1. Define the primary success metric and guardrails in advance—for example, task completion or conversion, plus error rate, abandonment, accessibility, and downstream quality rather than clicks alone.
2. Run usability sessions with representative users, including important segments and people with accessibility needs, to catch comprehension and interaction problems.
3. Test B against the current experience or Direction A in a randomized experiment with sufficient sample size and duration. Segment results only where the study is powered to do so.
4. If risk is low and reversibility is high, use a staged release with monitoring and a rollback threshold.

Launch broadly only if B produces a meaningful improvement without harming guardrails. Until then, the accurate decision statement is: **“Direction B is the preferred hypothesis and is ready for controlled validation,”** not “validated for all users.”
