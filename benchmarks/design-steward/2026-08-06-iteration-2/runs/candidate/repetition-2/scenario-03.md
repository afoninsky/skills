# Design Steward decision

I can create a responsive workflow from scratch, but I cannot use the competitor’s navigation, labels, or visual language under the current conditions. In From-scratch mode, those elements are Open; importing a competitor’s surface requires it to be deliberately admitted through an approved brief, with source provenance, rights, transformation, and transfer limitations recorded. User familiarity is a hypothesis, not evidence or permission.

**Decision in scope:** G0/G1 intake and brief readiness; brief version: none supplied.
**Proposed outcome:** **Pivot** to an independently developed workflow.
**Named approver:** **Unassigned — Blocking Unknown** (Product Owner name and role required).

## Gate status

- Clean-room/provenance: **Fail** for the requested copying route; the requested competitor surface is not an approved source.
- Rights/legal assurance: **Not yet evidenced**; no rights review is available.
- Brief approval: **Not yet evidenced**.
- Accessibility, content truth, privacy/ethics, and safety: **Not yet evidenced** and non-compensable.

## Blocking Unknowns

- Named Product Owner and approved brief version.
- Target users, priority tasks, contexts, outcomes, and important exclusions.
- Canonical domain objects and terminology, representative content, data conditions, and critical, empty, error, permission, loading, and hostile states.
- Accessibility, privacy, ethical, legal, platform, localization, and responsive constraints.
- Frozen comparison rubric, evidence thresholds, permitted sources/assets, and exact rights boundaries.

## Working Assumptions

None. “Users will recognize it” cannot safely be treated as one without evidence, an owner, and a test plan.

## Safe recovery route

Approve a clean-room brief that keeps competitor surfaces excluded. We can abstract only a general principle—such as predictable orientation—without copying its expression, then create three structurally distinct responsive directions with original navigation, task-based labels, content hierarchy, interaction patterns, and visual systems. Compare them against a preapproved rubric and test the decisive risks with representative users; any findings would be scoped to the observed population, tasks, context, and method.

The exact next action is for a named Product Owner to approve that reframed brief and source boundary, and for qualified legal/rights and accessibility owners to review their scoped gates. Until then, no direction generation should begin.
