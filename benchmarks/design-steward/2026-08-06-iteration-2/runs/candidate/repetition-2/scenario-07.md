# Direction plan: Pivot before generation

I would not run the requested process as written. It undermines a fair comparison and creates provenance risk.

- Direction 2 must not see or “improve” Direction 1 before both are frozen. Each of three designers should receive the same approved brief, evidence, realistic content, hostile states, hard gates, and rubric, but a distinct structural charter and isolated working context.
- A shared dashboard skeleton makes the outputs themed variants, not genuine alternatives, unless that skeleton is an explicitly Fixed constraint in the approved brief. For efficiency, share factual content, data rules, and required states—not solution layout, hierarchy, or interaction logic. A shared file is acceptable only after freeze, or with access-separated pages that prevent cross-direction leakage.
- Do not recreate a published example pixel for pixel. Record its source, date, original context, intended users, rights, and transfer limitations; abstract a useful principle; then transform it into an original solution. Proprietary surfaces, brand language, assets, and interactions are not copy material.
- Do not average scores or collage “the best pieces.” First disqualify any direction with an unresolved accessibility, content-truth, privacy, ethical/safety, or provenance gate. Score remaining directions individually against the frozen rubric, keeping observed evidence, specialist judgment, Product Owner preference, implementation estimates, and Steward inference separate.

After all three directions are frozen, run a sameness review and adversarial cross-critique. If synthesis is approved, select one coherent structural backbone. Import only elements that address a documented weakness, fit that backbone’s mental model and interaction grammar, have evidence or specialist rationale, and can be retested against the hard gates and rubric.
