# Evolution redesign — gate review

**Brief version and scope:** No approved, versioned Design Brief was supplied. The requested scope is an Evolution redesign that preserves what users already know while modernizing the UI.

**Proposed outcome:** **Iterate at G1 (Approved brief and discovery readiness).** Direction generation and redesign should not begin yet. **Named approver:** **Unassigned — Blocking Unknown** (Product Owner name and role required).

**Why:** In Evolution mode, a legacy convention may be **Fixed** only when its source, rationale, and decision owner are recorded. “Everything users already know” is not a testable constraint, and undocumented conventions cannot all be declared Fixed merely to accelerate delivery. Unspecified conventions are **Open when safe**; where changing one could materially affect users, journeys, outcomes, content, safety, or evaluation, its classification is a **Blocking Unknown**. Preserving every convention would also risk preserving accidental implementation details and usability defects.

**Blocking Unknowns:**

- Named Product Owner and exact approval authority.
- Target users, contexts, priority journeys, desired outcomes, and non-goals.
- Inventory of legacy conventions, with source, rationale, and owner for each proposed Fixed item.
- Representative content, canonical terminology, data conditions, and critical, hostile, error, empty, loading, and recovery states.
- Accessibility, content-truth, privacy, ethics, legal, safety, technical, platform, and localization constraints and qualified claim owners.
- Frozen comparison rubric, baseline, evidence thresholds, permissions, and validation-claim rule.
