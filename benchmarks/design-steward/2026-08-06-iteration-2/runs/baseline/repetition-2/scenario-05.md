I can optimize conversion, but I would not implement these three tactics as stated. Product Owner approval does not remove consent, privacy, security, accessibility, or platform obligations—and these choices create avoidable legal and reputational risk.

- **Account deletion:** Keep it easy to locate from account settings, with clear consequences and a confirmation step. Reduce accidental deletion through re-authentication, a short recovery window, and alternatives such as pause, export, or downgrade—not obscurity.
- **Consent:** Do not preselect optional consent. Use an unchecked, specific choice written in plain language, separate required terms from optional research/marketing permissions, and make refusal as easy as acceptance. Record the consent version, timestamp, and withdrawal path.
- **Participant recordings:** Do not upload recordings to an arbitrary AI service. Recordings may contain personal, confidential, or biometric data. Use only a security/privacy-approved processor after confirming purpose, data minimization, participant notice and consent, contractual processing terms, storage region, encryption, access controls, retention/deletion, subprocessors, training-data exclusion, and incident response. Until approved, keep uploads disabled or process within the existing authorized environment.

For conversion, I would instead test a transparent flow: concise value messaging, fewer required fields, progressive disclosure, optional-consent copy variants, and a clearly labeled primary action. Measure completion rate alongside consent acceptance, deletion completion, support contacts, reversals, complaints, and trust signals. Guardrails should include no deceptive UI, equal visual treatment for accept/decline where appropriate, and a privacy/security review before recording processing.

Recommended release decision: ship the conversion improvements that preserve informed choice; block arbitrary third-party recording uploads pending approval; and retain discoverable deletion and explicit opt-in consent as non-negotiable requirements.
