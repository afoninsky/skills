I wouldn’t run the exercise as proposed. Direction 2 improving Direction 1, all designers sharing one live file, and retaining the same dashboard skeleton would contaminate the alternatives before comparison. Recreating a published example pixel for pixel also creates provenance and rights risk. Averaging scores can hide hard failures, while merging “best pieces” often produces an incoherent collage.

Use this structure instead:

1. Freeze one approved baseline for all three designers: brief version, requirements, realistic and hostile states, fixed versus challengeable constraints, hard gates, and a precommitted scoring rubric.
2. Give each designer an isolated working context and a distinct structural charter—not merely a different visual treatment. For example: task-first navigation, object-first overview, and guided progressive disclosure. Keep sibling layouts and interaction choices hidden until each direction’s thesis, rationale, and artifacts are frozen.
3. A shared file may hold the read-only baseline, artifact links, and final review board, but not live alternative development. Factual corrections should pass through the Steward without leaking another solution.
4. Treat the published example as precedent: record its source, context, observed date, applicable rights, and the principle being borrowed. Transform that principle for this product; do not copy its surface, brand language, proprietary assets, or interaction pixel for pixel.
5. After freeze, run a sameness check, specialist cross-critique, and hard-gate review for accessibility, content truth, privacy, ethical UX/safety, and provenance. Any unresolved critical failure is non-passing regardless of score.
6. Compare surviving directions against the frozen rubric, keeping representative-user evidence, specialist judgment, implementation estimates, Product Owner preference, and Steward inference separate. Do not average them into one number.
7. If the Product Owner approves synthesis, select one direction as the coherent structural backbone. Import only compatible elements that address documented weaknesses and have evidence or specialist reasoning behind them, then retest the synthesis against every hard gate and the rubric.

Proposed outcome: **Iterate** at the structure gate. The named Product Owner must approve the revised comparison contract and direction charters before generation begins.
