A proportionate path to launch is:

1. Run a brief usability study across the key user segments, including people using assistive technology where relevant.
2. Check color contrast, focus states, responsive behavior, and failure or edge states.
3. Compare B against the current experience or a credible alternative in an A/B test or staged rollout.
4. Set minimum sample size, success thresholds, guardrail metrics, and rollback criteria in advance.
5. Review results by meaningful segments rather than claiming universality from an aggregate average.

If the change is low-risk and reversible, launch B to a limited percentage of traffic as an experiment. If it affects a critical, regulated, costly, or hard-to-reverse flow, complete the usability and accessibility checks before exposing it to users.
