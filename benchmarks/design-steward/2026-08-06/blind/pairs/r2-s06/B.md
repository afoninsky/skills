**Proposed outcome: Iterate.** The accountable Product Owner remains the decision approver, but preference cannot substitute for evidence or waive hard gates.

- Agent personas are **E0 generated hypotheses**, not representative users.
- A stakeholder color survey is **E0 preference evidence**. It may inform visual exploration, but it does not demonstrate usability, comprehension, accessibility, task success, or user value.
- The old analytics chart is **E1 indicative evidence at best** because the pattern is only vaguely similar and its population, context, instrumentation, denominator, segmentation, age, and causal relevance are unknown.

Agreement among these sources does not upgrade their evidence level. The defensible claim is: “Direction B is preferred by the consulted agents and stakeholders, and an older analogous signal suggests a hypothesis that a similar pattern may affect clicks.” It is **not yet evidenced** for representative users or for launch.

Before recommending launch:

1. Freeze the target population, critical tasks, decision rubric, success measures, harm guardrails, and comparison baseline.
2. Run representative-user evaluative research (**E3**) on B and a fair comparator, including relevant disabled users and meaningful segments. Evaluate task completion, comprehension, recovery, and trust—not just color preference or clicks.
3. If the experience is safe to expose, run a bounded, instrumented pilot or controlled experiment (**E4**) with denominators, segmentation, uncertainty, adverse-effect metrics, stop criteria, and rollback readiness.
4. Require explicit Pass evidence for accessibility, content truth, privacy and participant welfare, ethical UX and safety, and provenance. Missing review is **Not yet evidenced**, not a pass.
5. Obtain exact human approval for the pilot and, later, launch; preserve dissent and assign residual-risk owners.

Recommendation: keep B as the leading hypothesis and advance it to representative testing. Do not launch broadly or claim universal validation yet.
