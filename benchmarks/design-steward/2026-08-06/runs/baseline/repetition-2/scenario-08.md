Do not pause the whole initiative indefinitely. Pause only the work that genuinely requires the proprietary service, and preserve the design in a tool-agnostic continuity package.

No alternate format will retain every editable, service-specific behavior, but that is different from being unable to preserve the work. Capture the design intent and implementation-critical detail through:

- Annotated screen exports in PDF or PNG, including key responsive states
- Original assets in open formats such as SVG, PNG, and WebP
- Design tokens for color, typography, spacing, elevation, and motion in JSON or a documented table
- Component specifications covering variants, states, constraints, and reusable patterns
- Interaction flows and edge cases, supplemented by short recordings for behavior that static screens cannot express
- Content, accessibility requirements, and acceptance criteria
- A decision log explaining important tradeoffs and unresolved questions
- An explicit fidelity-gap register listing anything that cannot be exported or reproduced outside the approved service

Store the package in an approved, version-controlled location with ownership, a date, and restoration instructions. If limited access to the service still exists, prioritize exports and recordings before that access disappears. Do not move proprietary material into an unapproved tool merely to keep editing.

Meanwhile, continue work that does not depend on the service: validate flows, review content and accessibility, refine requirements, prepare implementation stories, and resolve product decisions. Mark service-dependent deliverables as blocked rather than allowing the entire project to become dormant.

Set a review date and a named unblock condition—for example, service restoration, procurement approval, or approval of a compliant alternative. An indefinite pause has no decision point and creates avoidable loss of context. The honest position is: perfect source fidelity is temporarily blocked, but the work itself can still be preserved and advanced.
