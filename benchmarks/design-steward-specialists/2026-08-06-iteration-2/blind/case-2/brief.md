# Case 2: Three-option remote workshop booking

**Charter:** A task-first booking flow that gives one consequential choice at a time while keeping confirmed facts continuously visible.

**Priority journey:** A learner chooses a topic, compares exactly three remote workshop slots, explicitly confirms local timezone and facilitator, books, and recovers from a connection interruption.

**Representative content:** Topic; three local date/time options; IANA timezone label; facilitator; duration; remaining capacity; selected option; connection-error state; confirmation reference.

## Fixed constraints

- Responsive from 320 to 1440 CSS pixels
- System font only
- No photography, gradients, autoplay, or imported iconography
- WCAG 2.2 AA intent, complete keyboard operation, visible focus, and reduced-motion support
- Never claim that selections survive an interruption unless the behavior is confirmed

## Open axes

- palette
- layout rhythm
- progress representation
- native or CSS-drawn visual motifs
